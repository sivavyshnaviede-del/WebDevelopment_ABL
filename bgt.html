Event Rules & Guidelines

Follow these rules to make the festival enjoyable for everyone.

  1. Students must register with correct details.
  2. Carry a valid college ID card.
  3. Report to the event venue at least 15 minutes early.
  4. Follow the instructions of coordinators and judges.
  5. Maintain discipline and respect all participants.
  6. Plagiarism, cheating, and disruptive behavior are prohibited.
  7. Participants must follow the specific rules of each competition.
  8. The organizing committee's announced decisions will apply to event disputes.
