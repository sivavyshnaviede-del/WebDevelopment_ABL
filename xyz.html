Essential Equipment Checklist

Pack smart before starting your journey.

🎒 Trekking Essentials

  • Comfortable trekking shoes
  • Backpack and water bottle
  • Weather-appropriate clothing
  • Cap and rain jacket
  • Map and navigation device

🏕 Camping Gear

  • Tent and sleeping bag
  • Flashlight or headlamp
  • Portable power bank
  • Reusable food containers
  • Waste collection bags

🩹 Personal Essentials

  • First-aid kit
  • Personal medications, if needed
  • Sunscreen and insect repellent
  • Emergency contact information
  • Extra drinking water and snacks

Safety Guidelines

Your safety comes first on every adventure.

🥾 Before the Trek

  • Choose a suitable difficulty level
  • Check weather and trail conditions
  • Carry enough water and food
  • Inform someone about your itinerary

⛰ During the Trek

  • Follow your guide and marked trails
  • Stay with your trekking group
  • Never approach wild animals
  • Follow emergency instructions

🌿 Protect Nature

  • Carry all waste back with you
  • Do not damage plants or trees
  • Avoid disturbing wildlife
  • Use designated camping areas
